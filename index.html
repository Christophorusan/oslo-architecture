03 / architecture

Same Benji-style idea: small sections, one job each. Click any layer to open the details without leaving the page.

inputs signal layer skills memory review measurement
01

Inputs

Market research, X posts, X replies, holder activity, vault flows, Discord decisions, BD notes, and product milestones.

  • Current: X source health and weekly lifecycle reporting.
  • Next: HyperEVM holder scanner and wallet/profile normalization.
02

Signal layer

Raw inputs become typed signals: holder lead, stablecoin allocator, HYPE/kHYPE interest, proof relevance, reply opportunity, retention risk.

  • Tables: lead_signals, holder_leads, x_posts, x_source_health.
  • Blocked until migrations 035/036 are applied.
03

Skills

Skills are the executable layer. They read signals, produce evidence, and write proposals or internal reports.

  • marketing.x-performance-audit audits Harmonix X.
  • bd.holder-lead-discovery scores wallet leads.
  • lifecycle.weekly-report posts lifecycle state to Discord.
04

Memory

Memory is separated by function: documents for operating truth, Supabase for structured signals, Discord for internal review history.

  • Docs live in brain/harmonix-sync/gtm/.
  • Structured lifecycle data lives in the 035/036 tables once applied.
05

Review

Oslo drafts. Humans decide. Internal reports and review cards go to MARKETING & COMMUNITY / #growth.

  • No automatic X, Telegram, Discord, or email DMs.
  • No public wallet balance disclosure.
06

Measurement

The system should learn from outcomes, not impressions alone.

  • Primary measures: approvals, replies, meetings, first deposits, repeat deposits, TVL retained, expansion deposits, withdrawal recovery.
  • Secondary measures: X proof density, source health, report cadence, and time from signal to action.

04 / lifecycle loop

01

Discover

Find HYPE, kHYPE, USDC, vault users, X conversations, partner signals, and allocator attention.

02

Qualify

Score by fit, timing, proof relevance, risk, recency, and relationship path. Attach evidence to every score.

03

Draft

Generate onboarding, activation, retention, win-back, and PMM launch touchpoints. Drafts only.

04

Review

Post internal report cards to Discord. Humans approve, edit, reject, or postpone.

05

Measure

Track reply, meeting, deposit, repeat deposit, TVL retained, expansion, withdrawal recovery, and approval quality.
